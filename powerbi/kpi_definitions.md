# 📘 KPI Definitions & Business Glossary — Orange Morocco Analytics

This document defines the key performance indicators (KPIs), business logic, and dimensions used in the **Orange Morocco Commercial Performance Dashboard**.

---

## 🔑 Executive Metrics Glossary

| Metric Name | Unit | Business Definition | Strategic Objective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Net Revenue** | MAD | Sum of net invoiced sales revenue after commercial discounts. | Benchmark core top-line performance against corporate revenue objectives. |
| **ARPU** | MAD | Average revenue per subscriber/transaction across product lines. | Track customer account value and cross-sell expansion effectiveness. |
| **Gross Margin %** | % | Percentage of revenue retained after direct product fulfillment costs. | Monitor portfolio profitability across Enterprise Cloud vs. Prepaid lines. |
| **Target Attainment %** | % | Ratio of realized revenue to planned annual regional targets. | Evaluate regional commercial quota execution and sales channel throughput. |
| **Revenue at Risk** | MAD | Total net revenue associated with contracts flagged with Churn Risk > 70. | Guide proactive B2B account retention interventions before contract expiry. |
| **High Churn Risk %** | % | Percentage of total revenue generated by accounts at high risk of departure. | Quantify commercial exposure to customer attrition across key accounts. |
| **Average NPS** | Score (1-10) | Average customer promoter score collected during quarterly touchpoints. | Gauge customer satisfaction, service delivery, and brand loyalty. |

## 🏢 Business Dimensions & Taxonomy

### 1. Geographic Coverage (Regions)
* **Casablanca-Settat:** Core commercial hub; high Enterprise Cloud & B2B volume.
* **Rabat-Salé-Kénitra:** Government, administrative, and premium postpaid concentration.
* **Marrakech-Safi:** Hospitality, tourism, and expanding retail network.
* **Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceïma:** Industrial, logistics, and port infrastructure accounts.
* **Fès-Meknès & Souss-Massa:** Regional retail expansion corridors.

### 2. Product Line Categorization
* **Enterprise Cloud & 5G Solutions:** High-ARPU B2B infrastructure & cloud services.
* **Fibre Optique High-Speed:** Fixed broadband connectivity for residential and SME clients.
* **Postpaid Mobile (Offre Yo / Mobile):** Monthly recurring voice & data mobile plans.
* **Smart Router & Hardware Add-ons:** Customer premises equipment (CPE) sales.
* **Prepaid Recharge & Pass:** Pay-as-you-go mobile top-ups and short-term data passes.

## 🎯 Strategic Thresholds
* **Churn Risk Level:**
  * **Low:** Risk Score $< 0.30$
  * **Medium:** Risk Score $0.30 - 0.60$
  * **High:** Risk Score $> 0.60$ (Triggers account management review)
